Image shatter

Click the photo. It turns into pixel art in a ripple from where you clicked, then shatter breaks it apart one pixel-art block at a time and snaps it back. Switch the mode to Shatter straight away and the photo stays as it is: the pixel art is only made behind the scenes, so the pieces that fly off are pixel art. Pick another photo, or drop in your own. This page loads dist/foopixel-fx.min.js. When you shatter an <img>, <canvas> or <video>, it breaks into the element's real pixels, one per cell, and each cell keeps showing the image until it breaks off. Pass pixels to take the colours from another image instead, which is how Shatter straight away keeps the photo while the pieces fall as pixel art. Cells are 6px × size, so this page draws its pixel art at the same block size and passes size: block / 6, and each block becomes one falling pixel. See-through parts of a PNG leave holes.

// any <img>, <canvas> or <video> shatters into its own pixels
img.addEventListener("click", (e) => {
  PixelFX.burst(img, { style: "shatter", origin: { x: e.clientX, y: e.clientY } });
});

<!-- or with no JavaScript at all -->
<img src="photo.jpg" data-pixel-burst="shatter" data-pixel-burst-size="2">

Images from another site can only be read if they're served with CORS headers and loaded with crossorigin="anonymous". If they can't be read, they shatter in the burst colours instead. Photos are from Lorem Picsum.
