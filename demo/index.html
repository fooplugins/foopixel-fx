Pixel hover effects

Four treatments for the upload drop area and CTA buttons, taken from the pixels breaking off the cloud in the logo: square, two sizes, blue shading to cyan. Hover, tab to, or drag a file over each one.

Reduced motion is on for this device, so the effects stay off.

Breakaway

Pixels detach from every edge and step outward, the same gesture as the logo, shading blue to cyan across the element. On buttons they peel off the whole pill in the button's own colour. The closest match to the mark.

Uplink

Pixels climb the sides of the drop area, turn in to the upload icon, and one leaves through the top. It tells the upload story, at the cost of a little more movement. Buttons fizz with pixels rising through the fill.

Dither

A hidden pixel grid lights up under the cursor and fades behind it, shading blue to cyan across the area like the logo. Buttons get one pixelated sweep on entry. The most responsive to the pointer.

Trace

Two short pixel trails weave along the border, each led by a pulsing head with a pixel hopping around it, and shed the odd pixel outward as they go. Reads clearly on both the dashed outline and the pill buttons, and works as a loading state too.
